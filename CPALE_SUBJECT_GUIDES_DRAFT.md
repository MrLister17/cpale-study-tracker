# Draft subject study guides for owner review

These six outlines are study-method drafts grounded in the supplied PRC TOS, effective October 2022. They are not approved lessons or an update of current law, tax rates, or accounting standards. The site keeps shared guides unpublished until the owner checks the topic labels, adds authoritative sources, and approves each entry. The linked [source PDF](https://www.prc.gov.ph/sites/default/files/2022-30%20BOA%20TOS%20Final.pdf) remains the scope reference.

## Financial Accounting and Reporting (FAR) · 54 extracted outcomes

**Coverage path:** financial-reporting framework and accounting process → presentation of statements → cash and financial assets → nonfinancial assets → liabilities and contingencies → equity → other reporting frameworks. Verify the exact outcome list and page references in the owner review queue.

**Study routine for each outcome:** read the applicable standard and class notes, write a one-paragraph recognition and measurement rule in your own words, work one entry or presentation problem, then explain how the resulting amount appears in the financial statements. Build a compact comparison sheet for initial versus subsequent measurement and for recognition versus disclosure.

**Practice checks:** identify the transaction date, measurement basis, journal entry, adjusting entry, statement line, and note disclosure before calculating. After each problem, reconcile the answer to the trial balance or relevant statement. For conceptual outcomes, explain both a correct treatment and a common incorrect treatment.

**Review trigger:** if a quiz error is a classification or timing error, revisit the underlying rule and redo a new example after one day. Verify current PFRS/PAS requirements before approving any final note or question.

## Advanced Financial Accounting and Reporting (AFAR) · 59 extracted outcomes

**Coverage path:** partnerships and liquidation → joint arrangements → revenue arrangements and specialized transactions → business combinations and consolidation → other advanced reporting scenarios in the TOS. The PDF extraction currently labels this subject as one broad section, so editorial section mapping is a priority.

**Study routine:** draw the entities, parties, ownership interests, and transaction timeline before recording any amount. Separate the legal transaction from the accounting unit of account. Work an acquisition or consolidation worksheet with columns for each entity, adjustments, eliminations, and the final total.

**Practice checks:** state what is recognized, by whom, on which date, and at what measurement basis. For partnership changes, reconcile each partner's capital and settlement. For revenue, identify the contract, performance obligations, transaction price, allocation, and recognition timing. Reconcile the final answer to the financial-statement effect.

**Review trigger:** classify wrong answers as a scope error, ownership/percentage error, elimination error, or arithmetic error; practice a new problem of the same type after the next lecture review.

## Management Services (MAS) · 21 extracted outcomes

**Coverage path:** management accounting and cost behavior → cost-volume-profit, costing and budgets → standard-cost variances and performance measures → relevant-cost decisions → financial management, working capital, capital budgeting, risk, financing, and economics.

**Study routine:** start each problem with a decision statement: what the manager is choosing and what information changes between alternatives. Label every amount as relevant or irrelevant, fixed or variable, and cash or noncash where appropriate. Build a formula sheet only after deriving each formula from a sample problem.

**Practice checks:** test units and signs before solving; compare total contribution, incremental cash flow, and opportunity cost rather than relying on a memorized formula alone. For budgets and variances, reconcile the component variances to the total variance. For investment decisions, state the timing and discount rate assumptions.

**Review trigger:** a wrong decision despite correct arithmetic points to a relevance or timing error; redo the case with a different set of numbers.

## Auditing (AUD) · 86 extracted outcomes

**Coverage path:** ethics and assurance concepts → audit planning, risk, materiality, evidence, and sampling → internal controls and responses → substantive procedures by account or cycle → completion and reporting. The extraction has theory and practice sections, which should be verified against the PDF.

**Study routine:** for each assertion, connect a risk to a procedure, the evidence obtained, and the conclusion it supports. Draw a simple flow from engagement acceptance through opinion. Review standards using decision trees for report modifications and other auditor communications.

**Practice checks:** ask whether a proposed procedure tests existence, completeness, valuation, rights/obligations, or presentation; then identify its direction of testing. For audit reports, identify the condition, materiality, pervasiveness, and resulting opinion. Explain why another response would be insufficient.

**Review trigger:** for each missed item, note the assertion or decision-tree branch that was confused and practice another scenario before marking the topic comfortable. Verify current Philippine Standards on Auditing and ethics rules before publication.

## Regulatory Framework for Business Transactions (RFBT) · 156 extracted outcomes

**Coverage path:** obligations and contracts → other business transactions and commercial laws → business organizations → special laws in the current TOS, including consumer, competition, procurement, and insolvency topics. This subject has the most extracted outcomes and needs proportional study time.

**Study routine:** make a rule card for each legal concept: elements, parties, required form, exception, remedy, and an example. Compare related rules in a table, then apply them to a short fact pattern. Maintain a list of provisions amended since the TOS date for owner verification.

**Practice checks:** identify the governing law, legally significant facts, element-by-element application, exception, and conclusion. Avoid choosing an answer from a memorized phrase without checking the fact pattern. For organization questions, separate liability, management authority, and registration requirements.

**Review trigger:** if an answer turns on an exception, add the exception to the rule card and revisit it in a spaced review. The owner must verify all current statutes, rules, and source rights before publishing content.

## Taxation (TAX) · 86 extracted outcomes

**Coverage path:** principles and remedies → income taxation → transfer taxes → business taxes → excise and documentary stamp taxes → local and preferential taxation. The PDF extraction carries some section headings into adjacent outcomes; the owner must correct those labels before approval.

**Study routine:** make separate sheets for taxable person, taxable event, tax base, rate, exemption, filing/payment deadline, and remedy. Draw a transaction timeline before computation. Keep every rate or threshold tied to an official source and an effective date; do not treat an old review-book rate as current.

**Practice checks:** identify the tax type and taxpayer before computing. Work from gross amount to exclusions, deductions, tax base, credit, and amount due, documenting which rules are assumed. Cross-check legal status and deadlines against current BIR/PRC materials before using a result in a shared answer key.

**Review trigger:** distinguish an incorrect rule from a wrong base, rate, deadline, or arithmetic step. Rework the same transaction with a changed fact after a short interval. No tax-rate question is published until its effective date is verified.

## How these guides become student content

The owner reviews a TOS outcome, writes a specific concise summary and lecture/practice prompt in the protected review queue, cites the relevant authoritative source, and publishes it. The owner then reviews at least three original multiple-choice questions for that outcome. Only published records appear in students' shared guide and quiz views. The study planner already schedules every extracted outcome; unapproved outcomes remain clearly identified until this process is complete.
