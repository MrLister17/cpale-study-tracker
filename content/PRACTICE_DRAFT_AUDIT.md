## Review workflow

1. A qualified CPA subject reviewer checks each stem, four choices, answer, explanation, topic mapping, effective date, and current primary source. Recheck TAX and RFBT near the exam.
2. The owner resolves findings and publishes an item in the protected review queue. A draft remains private until then.
3. A second reviewer samples published answer keys and checks explanations in the student quiz.

The quiz builder can already draw custom practice, a six-subject diagnostic, and a subject mock without repeated questions. Subject mocks use the PRC TOS group allocations for all six subjects. Every assembled quiz reports the actual question count and missing group coverage; the current draft pool cannot make a complete subject mock even if every item is eventually approved. Personal questions remain unreviewed practice and do not raise the reviewed-answer evidence score.
