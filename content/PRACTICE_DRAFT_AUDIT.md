Counts and tags alone do not establish accuracy or representative coverage.

## Review workflow

1. A qualified CPA subject reviewer checks each stem, four choices, answer, explanation, topic mapping, difficulty and cognitive-level tag, effective date, and current source. Recheck TAX and RFBT near the exam.
2. The owner resolves findings and publishes an item in the protected review queue. A draft remains private until then.
3. A second reviewer samples published answer keys and checks explanations in the student quiz.

The quiz builder can draw custom practice, a six-subject diagnostic, and a subject mock without repeated questions. Subject mocks use the [PRC TOS](https://www.prc.gov.ph/sites/default/files/2022-30%20BOA%20TOS%20Final.pdf) group allocations and target its 30% easy, 40% moderate, 30% difficult mix when tagged items permit it. The assembly reports actual question count, missing group coverage, untagged items, and difficulty shortfalls; it does not mark an incomplete mix as representative. The current draft pool cannot make a complete subject mock even if every item were eventually approved. Personal questions remain unreviewed practice and do not raise the reviewed-answer evidence score.

New draft explanations were checked against relevant source pages from the [IFRS Foundation](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/), [IAASB](https://www.iaasb.org/publications), [Philippine SEC](https://www.sec.gov.ph/revised-corporation-code-2/revised-corporation-code/), [BIR](https://www.bir.gov.ph/), and [ACCA technical articles](https://www.accaglobal.com/uk/en/student/exam-support-resources/fundamentals-exams-study-resources/f5/technical-articles.html). Structural validation checks unique stems, four distinct choices, TOS linkage, source URLs, and internal arithmetic. It does not replace expert answer-key review.
