# Sample guide content map

The six supplied October 2026 study-guide spreadsheets are planning references. They provide a sequence of themes and short focus lists, rather than complete lecture notes, worked solutions, or reusable question banks. The site now offers original study directions adapted from those references. The PRC Table of Specifications remains the list of assessable outcomes.

The student view gives every subject a sequence, a study method, and focus descriptions. Each PRC outcome also gets study steps. This is an actionable guide to **what to study and practice**. It is not a substitute for current standards, statutes, BIR issuances, lectures, or worked examples. Detailed notes and answer-keyed shared questions still require the owner’s editorial review under the original plan.

Source spreadsheets remain in the owner’s Downloads folder. No original workbook, lecture, handout, schedule grid, or question text is committed to the public repository.
